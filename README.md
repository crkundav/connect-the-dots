# Connect the Dots

A single-page, number-ordered dot-to-dot game for young kids, built for iPad touch.

Open `index.html` in Safari (or add it to the Home Screen). No build step or dependencies.

## How to play
- Choose a level: up to 10, 20 or 30 dots.
- Pick a picture: Star, House, Heart, Fish, Boat, Tree, Rocket, Cat, Ice Cream, Gem, Moon or Kite.
- Tap the dots in order (1, 2, 3…), or drag your finger through them. The next dot pulses orange.
- When you finish, the picture fills with color and confetti falls. You also earn a ⭐ on that picture for the level.

Controls: 🏠 home · 🔊 sound and spoken numbers · ↩️ undo · 🔄 restart.
